# Governance & Ethics

## Why Open-Source Governance?

For decades, real estate industry policy has been drafted behind closed doors by small, insulated committees—resulting in reactive compromises, technical blind spots, and unworkable statutory mandates. 

**Codex Praedium** (*Code of Real Property*) replaces secret committees with an **Open-Core Policy Model**. By publishing our working drafts, public comments, and legislative scorecards openly on GitHub, we ensure every rule is stress-tested by frontline practitioners, software architects, managing brokers, and legal experts before it reaches a statehouse floor.

---

## Three-Tier Open-Core Operating Model

To combine radical transparency with rigorous legislative execution, the Real Estate AI Task Force operates under a three-tier governance architecture modeled on modern open-source standards:

| Tier | Structure | Who Participates | Authority & Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Public RFC Portal** | Open to all licensed agents, brokers, engineers, consumers, and allied professionals. | Submit real-world edge cases, technical critiques, and line-by-line feedback on published drafts. All substantive submissions are archived publicly. |
| **Tier 2** | **Stakeholder Advisory Panels** | Invited specialists segmented into four dedicated tracks (Technical, Brokerage Ops, State Legal, Consumer). | Review public RFCs, identify statutory loopholes, and test implementation feasibility across firms of all sizes. |
| **Tier 3** | **Core Drafting Committee** | Task Force Chair, ARA Executive Leadership, and designated legal/policy counsel. | Holds final editorial and commit authority over model statutory text and official white papers to ensure legal precision and constitutional soundness. |

### Segmented Advisory Tracks

To prevent institutional inertia or business-side hesitation from silencing technical realities, Tier 2 advisory contributors are organized into four focused tracks:

1. **Technical & AI Systems Track:** Brokerage CTOs, ML engineers, and PropTech architects stress-testing human-in-the-loop checkpoints, audit logs, and API boundaries.
2. **Brokerage Operations & Supervision Track:** Managing brokers and compliance officers ensuring supervision rules scale cleanly across independent firms, teams, and national brokerages.
3. **State Legislative & Legal Track:** Real estate attorneys, former regulators, and ARA State Policy Chairs (prioritizing NY, FL, MD, CA, and TX).
4. **Consumer & Fiduciary Advocacy Track:** Consumer protection advocates and housing policy analysts evaluating buyer and seller liability risks.

## Code of Discourse

To ensure our work products command immediate respect from state legislators, attorneys general, and real estate commissioners, all Task Force participants must adhere to three non-negotiable rules:

> **1. Consumer Safety Over Protectionism:** All policy arguments must center strictly on **fiduciary duty, legal liability, data integrity, and consumer harm**. Rhetoric aimed at restricting competition, fixing fees, or blocking legitimate productivity tools is off-mission and will be archived out of policy tracks.

> **2. Strict Vendor Neutrality:** Task Force forums are for public policy, not product promotion. No participant—including Task Force leadership—may pitch, demo, or solicit business for commercial software products within Task Force channels or documents.

> **3. Separate Operations from Marketing:** Discussions regarding everyday marketing tools (such as virtual staging disclosures or copywriting prompts) are routed to practitioner best-practice threads so our statutory track remains laser-focused on unlicensed autonomous practice and broker supervision.

---

## Vendor Neutrality & Conflict Firewall

Effective technology policy requires builders who understand how autonomous AI systems work—paired with licensed brokers who carry real-world fiduciary liability. To maintain absolute institutional integrity, the Task Force enforces an ironclad **Vendor Neutrality Firewall**:

* **Zero Commercial Infrastructure Overlap:** The Task Force is an independent policy initiative of the American Real Estate Association (ARA). It does not share user data, member rosters, or communication platforms with any commercial brokerage or PropTech company (including MarketWhiffs, Inc.).
* **Model-Neutral Statutory Standards:** Our *Licensee-in-the-Loop* framework applies equally to every business model—traditional brick-and-mortar brokerages, virtual firms, teams, and digital platforms alike. Every licensed entity must meet the exact same human-supervision and fiduciary accountability standards, with zero proprietary carve-outs.
* **Strict Non-Solicitation:** Task Force repositories, working groups, and public comment portals are restricted exclusively to policy analysis. Any attempt to pitch software, recruit agents, or solicit consumer leads results in immediate removal.
* **Public Auditability:** Every version of our model legislation and technical standards is maintained in a public Git repository with a transparent commit history, allowing any broker, regulator, or competing trade association to verify that the text serves the public interest.
