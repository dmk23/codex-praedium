# Get Involved

The **Real Estate AI Task Force** operates as an open-source policy coalition. Rather than relying on closed-door committees or outside consultants, we invite frontline practitioners, technologists, managing brokers, attorneys, and consumer advocates to contribute directly to our statutory frameworks and technical standards.

## How to Submit Public Feedback (RFC Process)

Every draft of our model legislation and technical compliance standards is open for public review on GitHub:

1. **Review the Active Drafts:** Read the current statutory principles and checkpoints on the [Model Legislation](https://RealEstateAITaskForce.com/legislation/index.md) page.
1. **Identify a Specific Issue:** Focus your submission on a concrete statutory definition, technical edge case, or documented consumer harm scenario.
1. **Open a Structured RFC Issue:** Visit the [Codex Praedium Issues Portal](https://github.com), tag your submission with `RFC-Feedback`, and include:
1. **Jurisdiction / Context:** Which state law or brokerage workflow is affected.
1. **Consumer or Fiduciary Risk:** What harm occurs if the gap is left unaddressed.
1. **Proposed Amendment:** Specific language or technical guardrail you recommend.

> **Attribution & Confidentiality Options:** Public RFC submissions are archived transparently on GitHub. For enterprise engineering leaders or compliance officers joining our **Advisory Panels**, participation is available under **Chatham House Rule** (optional attribution) to respect corporate communications policies.

## Community Rules of Engagement

To maintain credibility with state legislators, attorneys general, and regulatory boards, all contributors must abide by our [Code of Discourse](https://RealEstateAITaskForce.com/governance/#rules-of-engagement-code-of-discourse):

- **Anchor in Consumer Protection:** Frame all arguments around fiduciary duty, legal accountability, and public safety—never commission protectionism or anti-competitive restrictions.
- **Zero Vendor Pitching:** Task Force forums and working groups are strictly non-commercial. Promoting, demoing, or soliciting leads for proprietary software products is prohibited.
- **Keep Marketing Separate:** Discussions regarding virtual staging disclosures, listing copy prompts, or everyday agent AI tools belong in general practitioner forums rather than statutory drafting tracks.

## Institutional & Association Collaboration

We welcome formal collaboration with state and local real estate associations, MLS organizations, and consumer protection groups. If your organization is drafting or evaluating state-level AI legislation, use our [3-Point Legislative Litmus Test](https://RealEstateAITaskForce.com/#the-3-point-legislative-litmus-test) as a shared baseline or open a collaborative working thread on [GitHub](https://github.com).
